# Supplier distress rubric

## Tiers

- **Critical** — immediate action: bankruptcy or insolvency filing, administration/receivership, missed payment or default, covenant breach, going-concern warning, credit downgrade to CCC or below, auditor resignation or restatement.
- **High** — downgrade by a rating agency, profit warning, credit-insurance withdrawal, plant closure, strike or outage lasting more than two weeks, regulatory investigation, loss of a key customer or contract.
- **Medium** — layoffs, reported supply delays, lengthening payment terms, small downgrade, repeated negative coverage with no hard financial trigger.
- **Low / none** — routine news, sector-wide stories, marketing and awards.

## Rules

1. Attribute an item to the supplier only when it is about the supplier itself — not its customers, competitors or the sector in general.
2. Weight official filings, rating-agency actions and exchange announcements above press speculation.
3. A Critical tier needs either one official filing/rating action or at least two independent reputable sources; otherwise cap the tier at High and say so.
4. Always record the evidence date and URL; un-cited items do not count.
5. When evidence is thin or stale, state the confidence as low instead of inflating the tier.
6. Score 0–100 for severity, where 0 is no signal and 100 is an active insolvency event.
